FAQ

Delivery questions

How fast is delivery?

The chain check runs in your own browser, so the file unlocks seconds after your transaction confirms — usually less than a minute after sending.

My hash says “not found”.

The transaction is probably still waiting for its first confirmation. Wait about 60 seconds and press the button again. If it still fails, use the email button — your hash is the proof.

Why does it ask for a hash?

It is the receipt. The page reads the payment on the Ethereum network itself — no account, no personal data, nothing stored on a server.

I paid but closed the tab.

Come back to this page with the same hash, or email it — the file can always be re-sent.

Which network did I need?

Ethereum (ERC-20) only, for now. USDT sent on TRON, BSC or any other chain cannot be recovered automatically.
