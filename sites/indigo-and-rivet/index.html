From mill to hem

Nine months on the loom

The denim is woven on shuttle looms in Okayama, then cut and sewn forty minutes from our door. We buy one roll at a time and run it until it is gone, so a pair bought in 2019 still fits the pair you order today.

We keep the range tight so every item earns its place — nothing is here to fill a gap on the shelf.
