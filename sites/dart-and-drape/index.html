How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Dart & Drape is a dresses label working from a 4-person workshop. We buy the viscose crepe first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Bias-cut panels on every stress point
  • Silk crepe de chine sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
