In the fitting room

Five minutes with an optician

Every order is checked before it leaves the bench: axis, pupillary distance, edge height, then a second pair of eyes on the coating. If it sits half a millimetre out it does not go in the box — we reset it, cut it again and ship it the same day.

We keep the range tight so every item earns its place — nothing is here to fill a gap on the shelf.

How fitting works
Why Frame & Field

Shopping without the noise

👓

Fitted, not just shipped

Bring your pupillary distance to the studio or send it with the order — we edge and mount the lenses here, in forty-eight hours.

🔧

Free adjustments for life

Nose pads, temples, a loose screw — walk in whenever they feel off. No receipt needed, not in year one, not in year five.

📦

Home try-on, five frames

Pick five frames, wear them for five days, send back whatever you did not keep. Shipping both ways is on us.

🛡️

Blue-light coating, standard

Every clear lens ships with anti-reflective and blue-light filtering at no extra cost — the coating is never an upsell.
