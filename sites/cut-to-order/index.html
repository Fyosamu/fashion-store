FAQ

Before you order

How does it fit?

Every product page carries a full measurement chart in centimetres and inches. If you are between two sizes, take the larger — the cut from your measurements has limited give.

What is your return policy?

Thirty days, unworn, tags on, no explanation needed. Return postage is on us inside the UK and EU; elsewhere it is deducted from the refund.

How should I wash it?

Machine wash cold with like colours and hang to dry. The individual paper pattern is the one thing that will not survive a hot tumble dry.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. Tracked worldwide, free over $120. UK and EU orders clear customs pre-paid; US orders under $800 are duty free.

Is it made in a factory?

No. Everything is cut and sewn in our own 5-person workshop in small runs, which is why some sizes sell out before others.
