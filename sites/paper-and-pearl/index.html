How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Paper & Pearl is a occasion boutique label working from a 11-person workshop. We buy the silk-georgette blend first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Hand-rolled hem on every stress point
  • Fine-gauge merino sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
