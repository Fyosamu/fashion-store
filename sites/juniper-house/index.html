How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Juniper House is a linen boutique label working from a 7-person workshop. We buy the herbal-dyed linen first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Plant-dye batches on every stress point
  • Cotton-linen slub sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
