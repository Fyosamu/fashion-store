In the blending room

Six weeks in the dark

Every batch rests forty-two days in the barrel room before it is bottled. The alcohol settles, the citrus softens and the cedar learns to stay. We blend on Mondays, bottle when the bench says it is ready, and write the batch number on by hand.

We keep the range tight so every item earns its place — nothing is here to fill a gap on the shelf.
