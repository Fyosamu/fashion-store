How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Cold Front is a women's outerwear label working from a 5-person workshop. We buy the 800-fill responsible down first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Baffle-box stitching on every stress point
  • Double-faced wool sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
