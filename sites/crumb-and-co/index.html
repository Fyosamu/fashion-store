The overnight

Thirty-six hours before it hits the counter

Flour, water, salt, wild yeast and time. Our levain is fed twice a night and the dough rests cold until the ovens are hot — which is why the crumb looks like that.

We keep the range tight so every item earns its place — nothing is here to fill a gap on the shelf.
