How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Malt Shop is a 50s soda-shop label working from a 4-person workshop. We buy the cotton gabardine first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Saddle-stitch yoke on every stress point
  • Cherry-print lawn sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
