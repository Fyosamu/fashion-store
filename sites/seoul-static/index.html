How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Seoul Static is a korean fashion label working from a 7-person workshop. We buy the cotton-blend suiting first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Clean-finished insides on every stress point
  • Fine-gauge knit sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
