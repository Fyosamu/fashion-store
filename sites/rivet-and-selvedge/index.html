How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Rivet & Selvedge is a denim label working from a 5-person workshop. We buy the 14oz Japanese selvedge first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Chain-stitch hem on every stress point
  • Shuttle-loom chambrade sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
