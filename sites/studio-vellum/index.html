How it is made

Forty pieces at a time, then we stop.

Studio Vellum is a refined basics label working from a 12-person workshop. We buy the long-staple cotton first and design around whatever arrives, rather than ordering fabric to fit a sketch.

  • Low-twist garment wash on every stress point
  • Brushed loopback sourced from a mill we have used since day one
  • Free repairs for the first three years, whatever went wrong
  • Nothing destroyed at the end of a season — leftovers become the next run
