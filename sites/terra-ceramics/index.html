No two are the same, on purpose

Each piece is thrown, trimmed, glazed and loaded into a kiln that holds forty — so the speckle, the pooling at the rim and the weight in your hand are yours alone. Small runs, signed underneath.

We keep the range tight so every item earns its place — nothing is here to fill a gap on the shelf.

Why Terra Ceramics
