Delivery

Three steps, no account needed

You pay in crypto, you get the folder. Nothing is stored on this site.

01

Pay the standing price

Send 19 USDT (14 USDT on the three entry templates) to the address on the buy page — USDT, ERC-20. The $49 list price stays permanently reduced, with no timer anywhere.

02

Paste your transaction hash

Drop the hash from Trust Wallet into the delivery page. It is your receipt: keep it and the folder stays yours to re-download at any time.

03

The file unlocks itself

Your browser reads the Ethereum network directly, confirms the amount and starts the download in seconds. It runs 24/7 — nobody has to send you anything.
